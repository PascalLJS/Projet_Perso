This game was discovered on an old development computer belonging to a small game studio.

According to the remaining files, the studio had been working on a Christmas game for years. The project was never officially released.

Then, without warning, the developers disappeared.

The company was eventually shut down, and most of the project was lost. Only a few files were recovered from the old development machines.

Among them was this build.

We don't know what happened to the developers, and we don't know why they left the game in this state.

What we do know is that something is wrong.

Parts of the game are missing.
Some features don't work.
Others appear to have been deliberately disabled.

We've managed to recover enough of the project to launch it, but we can't get any further.

So we've decided to leave it in your hands.
