Our reverse-engineering team has been going through the remaining files from the original development build.

Most of them are exactly what we expected to find.

Except for one.

We discovered a file that is considerably larger than the other scene files in the project.

At first, we assumed it was simply a corrupted or unfinished scene.

But after examining it more closely, we found something unusual.

The scene appears to contain content that isn't accessible from anywhere in the game.

There are no buttons.
No documented entrance.
No references from the other scenes.

It looks like the developers deliberately left it behind.

Even stranger...

The scene has a name:

"HiddenSecretScene"

We don't know what is inside.

We don't know why it was hidden.

And we don't know if the developers ever intended anyone to find it.

Your task: Find a way into HiddenSecretScene.
